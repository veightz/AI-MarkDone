# Narration Script

## Voice Direction

English voiceover. Confident, crisp, product-launch energy. Fast enough to feel urgent, with short pauses after each sentence. The subtitles should match the narration line-by-line.

## Script

ChatGPT can answer almost anything.
But serious work needs more than scrolling and copying.

Navigate long threads.
Keep your place.

Copy exactly what matters, with Markdown, code, formulas, and notes intact.

Save, search, and export your best answers when the work leaves the chat.

AI-MarkDone.
Read, save, export.
Stay in flow.
